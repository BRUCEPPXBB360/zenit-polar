Privacy Policy — Zenit Polar

Last updated: October 2026

Zenit Polar ("the app") is developed by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and what choices you have.

1. Information the app stores

Zenit Polar stores your translations, conversations, and custom ciphers on your device using Apple's storage framework (SwiftData). If you are signed in to iCloud, this data is also synchronized between your own devices (iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch) through your private iCloud account (Apple CloudKit and iCloud key-value storage). It is stored in your personal iCloud space, which only you can access; it is never uploaded to, or stored on, any server operated by us, and we cannot see it. You can turn this off at any time in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud. If you delete the app, local data is deleted with it.

2. Microphone and speech recognition

If you use the voice dictation feature, audio is processed using Apple's built-in Speech framework, which performs recognition on-device whenever possible. We do not record, store, or have access to your voice recordings. Microphone access is only requested when you actively tap the dictation button, and only for the duration of that recording.

3. Purchases and free trial

Zenit Polar includes a 24-hour free trial, after which a one-time in-app purchase unlocks the app for life. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we do not receive or store any payment information. The start date of your trial is stored on your device (in the Keychain and app storage) and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synchronized through your private iCloud account, so that reinstalling the app does not restart the trial. Zenit Polar contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

4. No account or personal data collection

Zenit Polar does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information.

5. Children's privacy

Zenit Polar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
